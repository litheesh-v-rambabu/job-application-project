Resume Tailor

AI tailors your stored resume to perfectly match any job description.

info How it works

Your base resume is stored in your n8n workflow. The AI reads the job description, identifies required keywords and skills, then rewrites your resume sections to maximize ATS match — while keeping everything truthful to your actual experience.

To update your resume: open n8n → Workflow 14 → Code node → edit RESUME text → Save.
