## Template 3 — Referral Request (Reaching Out to Someone at the Company)

Use this when you know someone — even loosely — who works at the company you are targeting. A referral increases your chances of getting an interview significantly.

---

**Subject:** Quick Ask — [Company Name] Referral for [Job Title] Role

Hi [First Name],

I hope you are doing well! I was browsing job openings and noticed that [Company Name] is hiring for a [Job Title] — a role that really excites me given my background in [your field].

I know you have been at [Company Name] for a while now, and I wanted to reach out to ask — would you be comfortable referring me for the position, or sharing a few thoughts about the team and what they look for?

A little about me: I am a Computer Science graduate student at the University at Buffalo with experience in [2–3 skills], and I have been working on [brief project or achievement]. I genuinely believe I would be a strong fit.

I completely understand if this is not something you are able to do, and I would not want to put you in an awkward position. Even a quick insight about the role or the hiring process would be incredibly helpful.

Either way, I would love to catch up soon — it has been a while!

## Quick Tips for All Emails

| Do | Do Not |
|---|---|
| Address by first name | Say "To Whom It May Concern" |
| Keep it under 200 words | Write long paragraphs |
| Mention one specific thing about the company | Send a generic copy-paste with no personalisation |
| Attach your resume | Forget the attachment |
| Send from buffalo.edu email | Use a casual personal email |
| Follow up once after 7 days | Follow up more than twice |
| Proofread before sending | Send with typos or wrong company name |

## What the AI Will Do Automatically

When you use the **Outreach** page in the app:

1. Select a job from your tracker
2. The AI reads your resume and the job description
3. It personalises one of these templates — fills in your skills, the company name, a specific detail about the role
4. You review and edit the draft
5. Click **Send via Gmail** or copy it to send manually

The AI uses Google Gemini (best natural writing style) to fill in the template so it sounds like you wrote it personally — not a generic template.
